# Third-party notices

Copyright 2026 GeneJie199 applies to project-owned material; it does not replace
third-party copyright. Dependency and asset license texts retain their own terms.

The generated `third-party-inventory.json` records exact Go module versions, license
file paths and SHA-256 values. `LICENSES/third-party/` preserves discovered texts;
`third-party.cdx.json` supplies a CycloneDX license inventory. License recognition is
evidence plus warnings, not an automated legal compatibility opinion. Review any
NOASSERTION or unavailable-source exception before distribution.

Lucide's vendored browser asset includes ISC and Feather-derived notices in each
product's `internal/web/static/vendor/LUCIDE_LICENSE.txt`; these are preserved and
copied into the inventory. No external font or icon CDN is introduced by v2. Go's
runtime license and patent notice are included for binary redistribution. Screenshots
and project assets retain their existing repository attribution/provenance; no new
ownership claim is made for them.

Historical v1 binaries/protocol archives referenced by `suite-manifest.json` remain
Apache-2.0 with their transitive dependency notices. Offline aggregates include the
license tree, inventory, source-license metadata and NOTICE; their individual licenses
do not become AGPL merely because the development launcher is AGPL.

See [copyright audit](docs/v2-license-audit.md) for scope and limitations.
