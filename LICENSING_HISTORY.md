# Licensing history

Transition date: 2026-10-30. Effective source line: v2.0.0 development and later
Community Core, AGPL-3.0-only. No v2 GA release is asserted by this document.

Previously published v1.x and earlier Apache-2.0 releases keep their original grants.
Tags, history, published archives and their checksums are not deleted or rewritten;
no historical user's Apache rights are revoked. The original Apache text and notices
are retained under `LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt`. Future releases must identify the exact
source revision, license and component versions in release metadata.

The checked-in stable suite manifest still references immutable v1.0.0 Apache
component binaries. Packaging such historical independent components does not
relicense them. Development source and a historical binary have distinct artifact
identities and license metadata; see `LICENSE_METADATA.json` and third-party inventory.

Commercial/OEM arrangements are separate written grants from the rights holder for
specified versions and rights; see [commercial policy](COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md).
