# Commercial and OEM licensing policy — negotiation draft

This document is a policy/scope draft, not a signed license, offer acceptance,
purchase contract or authorization to disregard AGPL. A separate written agreement
must be signed by the actual rights holder and customer. Legal review recommended.

Intended customers include software vendors, systems integrators, OEMs, white-label
platforms, embedded products and customer-delivery businesses needing rights beyond
their applicable Community obligations. A negotiated license may permit modification,
closed-source derivative works, embedding, redistribution, rebranding, white-labeling,
commercial resale, downstream resale and customer delivery within its agreed scope.

Commercial use alone does not require purchasing a commercial license. AGPL remains
available when its applicable conditions are satisfied. Removing or replacing branding
is not itself an extra restriction under AGPL; permission to use official branding is
a separate trademark matter. OEM agreements can separately include brand/support rights.

Scope must identify licensed versions, entities, products, distribution rights,
customer deliveries, third-party exclusions, term, fees, termination and source rights.
Third-party components remain under their own licenses and cannot be relicensed by us.
No automatic unlimited warranty, liability, indemnification, SLA or perpetual support
is promised. Support/services require explicit terms. The repository names
GeneJie199 as its current copyright holder; no company ownership is asserted.

Contact the maintainer through the repository's public contact path. Do not submit
credentials, contracts or personal data in a public issue.
